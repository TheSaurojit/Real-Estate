Real Estate ERP Studio Enterprise Edition

Property Lifecycle, Dual-Ledger Accounting & Site Operations Platform

Executive Presentation Summary

A Unified Operating System for Property Developers & Builders

This software replaces scattered spreadsheets, offline paper ledgers, and disconnected accounting systems. It connects every stage of real estate development: land registry and RERA projects, dynamic flat bookings, dual-ledger accounting (taxable banking vs. cash), on-site customization job sheets, construction material tracking, and automated generation of 12 legal and billing documents.

Functional Modules Breakdown

Explore each module, its practical real-world purpose, and key capabilities.

MOD-01

Multi-Company & Project Workspaces

Enterprise multi-tenancy & statutory land registry

Manage multiple developer companies and individual housing projects from one portal, while keeping statutory land documents cleanly segregated.

Land Particulars: Tracks Daag No., Patta No., Holding No., Mouza, and Pogonah.
RERA Compliance: Flags projects as Registered, Applied, or Exempted with numbers.
Context Switcher: Instant toggling between head office and specific site workspaces.
Eliminates mixing records between different corporate companies or sites.
MOD-02

Banking & RERA Escrow Master

Statutory escrow segregation & cash accounts

Strictly partitions developer funds into dedicated bank accounts to comply with banking regulations and statutory housing laws.

Project Escrow Accounts: Dedicated RERA bank accounts for customer collections.
General Bank Accounts: Corporate operating, head-office, and administrative accounts.
Cash-in-Hand Register: Real-time tracking of internal site cash reserves.
Prevents illegal co-mingling of project funds and protects during audits.
MOD-03

Dynamic Flat & Unit Bookings

On-the-fly inventory, automated pricing & KYC

Allows sales reps to book flats on the fly with comprehensive pricing logic without rigid inventory obstacles.

Flexible Inventory: Enter Block, Floor, Unit No., and SBUA directly at booking.
Auto Pricing Matrix: Unit base rate + Parking + Transformer + Amenities.
Customer KYC & JDA: Tracks PAN, Aadhaar, and Landowner JV flags.
Cuts flat booking time to under 2 minutes with automated math verification.
MOD-04

Dual-Ledger Accounting Engine

Taxable Bank (Escrow) vs. Cash Ledger balance

Separates real estate transactions into official registered agreement bank receipts and non-taxable cash vouchers.

Money Receipts (MR): Official bank transaction receipts with GST compliance.
Receipt Vouchers (RV): Cash vouchers for on-site client alterations.
Rebalance Wizard: Adjusts excess banking collections to cash accounts cleanly.
Keeps official tax audits clean while giving management 100% total cash visibility.
MOD-05

Customization Job Sheets

Add-ons vs. Dislodges (Material + Labor)

Bridges the gap between on-site client modification requests and the billing office.

Add-on Works: Extra AC lines, premium lights (material + labor).
Dislodges (Deductions): Credit for buyer-supplied tiles + adjustment fees.
Live Bill Rollup: Updates supplementary value on the customer statement instantly.
Stops unbilled on-site builder losses from informal client modifications.
MOD-06

Home Loan & Bank Finance Tracker

Lending bank synchronization & disbursement

Monitors mortgage and home loan files across nationalized and private lending institutions.

Loan Pipeline: Tracks Applied, Sanctioned, Disbursed, or Rejected.
Lending Details: Stores loan account no., sanction dates, and bank branches.
Auto-Sync: Incoming bank disbursements automatically update loan records.
Prevents flat registrations from happening before all loan funds have been disbursed.
MOD-07

Legal Agreements & Registry Deeds

Bayna-nama & Sub-Registrar Office deeds

Maintains formal legal documentation milestones from initial booking agreement to final conveyance.

Sale Agreement (Bayna-nama): Logs serial numbers, dates, and agreement value.
Sale Deed Tracking: Records SRO office, deed serial no., and registry dates.
Ledger Realignment: Automatically syncs tax values to formal agreement amounts.
Provides an iron-clad legal audit trail for every registered property.
MOD-08

Cancellations & 3-Way Split Refunds

Lien protection & multi-party payout settlement

Eliminates developer liability when a buyer cancels after availing a bank home loan.

Penalty Retention: Deducts agreed contractual cancellation charges.
Bank Lien Repayment: Allocates refunds to clear lending bank loans first.
Customer Refunds: Splits remaining balances across bank and cash ledgers.
Protects builders from bank litigation and unauthorized cash refund claims.
MOD-09

Site Expenses & Stock Transfers

Material procurement & inter-site inventory shifting

Tracks raw material consumption, contractor bills, and materials moved between active construction sites.

Site Procurement: Logs cement, steel, bricks, and contractor bills.
Inter-Project Transfers: Tracks material sent from Site A to Site B with vehicle challans.
Cost Allocation: Automatically reallocates material expense to destination site.
Prevents material theft, ghost invoices, and distorted project costs.
MOD-10

Executive Reports & Profitability

Live ROI, inventory velocity & payment aging

Delivers instant high-level analytics for directors and developers without waiting for month-end audits.

Profitability Analysis: Real-time Revenue vs. Direct Expenses & Net Margin %.
Customer Aging Matrix: Overdue dues grouped into 0-30, 31-60, 61-90, 90+ days.
Banking Escrow Flow: Live net liquid position across bank and cash balances.
Empowers management with immediate data for smarter cash-flow planning.
MOD-11

Document Hub & 12 Print Templates

1-click corporate letters, NOCs & tax receipts

Automates the generation of every standard legal and financial document used in real estate transactions.

Letters: Booking Confirmation, Allotment, Demand Notes, Possession.
Financial Statements: Final Bill, Money Receipts, 360° Account Statements.
Corporate Styling: Formatted with company headers, logos, and signatures.
Eliminates manual Word document typing and formatting errors completely.
MOD-12

Auto-Numbering & Granular RBAC

Thread-safe codes & 5 pre-configured roles

Enterprise security backbone ensuring strict audit trails and role-specific operational permissions.

Sequential Auto-Numbers: Formats like SSI/BK-1001, SSI/RCD1001 without collisions.
5 System Roles: Super Admin, Admin, Manager, Accountant, Site Engineer.
20+ Granular Permissions: Restricts viewing or editing of sensitive financial ledgers.
Guarantees bulletproof data privacy and tamper-proof financial numbering.

Technology Stack & Architecture

Built on Laravel, styled with Tailwind CSS, with dynamic client interactions powered by Alpine.js. Uses database transactions and pessimistic locking for auto-numbering to prevent voucher duplicate errors during concurrent peak booking hours. Fully modular, responsive, and mobile-friendly for on-site managers.
